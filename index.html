Heat and rainfall awareness

BASIC GUIDANCE · NOT AN OFFICIAL WARNING

Once current weather loads, this panel will show general heat-awareness guidance.

  • Drink safe water regularly, especially during hot periods.
  • Reduce strenuous outdoor activity during the hottest part of the day.
  • For heavy rain, avoid crossing flooded roads or flowing water.

Current conditions and forecast estimates are displayed separately.

Community vulnerability

INDEX NOT YET CALCULATED

A credible Community Climate Vulnerability Index requires transparent, local evidence on:

  • Hazard: heat, intense rainfall and flooding.
  • Exposure: people, homes, farms and infrastructure in affected areas.
  • Coping capacity: access to water, electricity, health services, transport and other resources.

No numerical vulnerability score is presented until suitable data and a documented method are available.

Data transparency

  • Current weather: retrieved from the configured weather service through this website's API endpoint.
  • Forecast: retrieved from a separate forecast API endpoint and aggregated into daily estimates.
  • Official alerts: not integrated.
  • Electricity and water: no verified local feed or community-report database connected.
  • Vulnerability index: not calculated; methodology and local datasets are still required.
